# Wave 1 value and usefulness signal

## Classification

**SUPPORTED INTEREST**, with material adoption constraints.

## Direct response evidence

- Usefulness mean 3.89/5 and median 4/5.
- Ratings were 5 from 4/9, 4 from 1/9, 3 from 3/9, and 2 from 1/9.
- Most useful capability: affected repositories 5/9, centralized evidence 2/9, historical reconstruction 1/9, missing/uncertain evidence 1/9.
- Non-identifying free text requested consolidation, compatibility/testing guidance, verification, troubleshooting, and affected-repository prioritization.
- Follow-up willingness: Yes 1/9, Maybe 4/9, No 4/9.

## Analyst interpretation

The sample supports interest in the decision area and especially in identifying the affected set. It does not establish validated demand, product-market fit, ROI, adoption intent, purchase intent, or changed decisions. The strongest negative constraints are lack of trust and integration effort (5/9 each), false positives and security/privacy (4/9 each), and review burden (3/9).

The value proposition should remain narrow: assemble traceable evidence and help scope further investigation. It should not promise automated migration decisions, savings, or complete coverage.

## Required next evidence

An authorized follow-up should ask participants to use a bounded PULSE case and record whether it changes affected-repository selection, evidence search, confidence, or next action. Measure time and review work only after the baseline workflow is observed. One usefulness rating cannot substitute for that exercise.
