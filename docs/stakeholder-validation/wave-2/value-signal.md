# Wave 2 value and usefulness signal

## Classification

**SUPPORTED INTEREST**, with stronger trust constraints and no observed product value.

## Direct evidence

- Cumulative usefulness mean 3.86/5 and median 4/5, nearly unchanged from Wave 1 mean 3.89/5.
- Ratings: 5 from 5/14, 4 from 3/14, 3 from 5/14, and 2 from 1/14.
- Most useful: affected repositories 5/14, central evidence 4/14, missing/uncertain evidence 3/14, historical reconstruction 1/14, and investigation report 1/14.
- No addition selected affected-repository identification as most useful; central evidence and uncertainty each received 2/5.
- Follow-up willingness: Yes 3/14, Maybe 4/14, No 7/14.

## Interpretation

Interest is stable across waves, while preferred value is more distributed than Wave 1 suggested. Trust is the leading barrier at 9/14. The evidence does not establish demand, product-market fit, ROI, adoption intent, purchase intent, time savings, or changed decisions.

The defensible proposition remains: assemble traceable evidence, expose missingness, and support a bounded investigation report or affected-set decision. Automated remediation and complete coverage remain unsupported.

## Next evidence
