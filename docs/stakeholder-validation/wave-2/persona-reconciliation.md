# Wave 2 persona and decision-owner reconciliation

## Evidence

- Cumulative roles: 8 software engineers, 2 full-stack engineers, 2 security/software-supply-chain engineers, 1 data/AI/ML engineer, and 1 backend engineer.
- Direct dependency-upgrade experience: 10/14.
- Explicit multi-repository scope: 7/14; four report one repository and three are unsure.
- Final owner: technical lead 4/14, shared decision 3/14, engineering manager 2/14, individual engineer 2/14, and platform/dependency team, repository/service owner, and security team 1/14 each.

## Reconciliation

**Investigator persona: `PARTIALLY_CONFIRMED`.** The cumulative evidence supports an engineer or technical investigator who evaluates dependency-upgrade impact for one or more repositories. Wave 2 adds broader repository scopes and a platform/dependency-team owner response, but it does not validate a dedicated platform persona, recurring frequency, seniority, or authority.

**Final decision owner: `MIXED`; single-role hypothesis remains `CONTRADICTED`.** Technical lead is the largest category at 4/14 but does not approach a majority. Investigator, reviewer, repository/service owner, platform/dependency team, manager, security owner, and final decision owner must remain separable.

## Proposed wording

“An engineer or technical investigator evaluating dependency-upgrade impact for one or more repositories, who assembles evidence and may advise a technical lead, repository owner, platform team, manager, security team, or shared decision group.”

This wording remains provisional. A future task must test who initiates scope, who may inspect private evidence, who reviews candidates, and who approves remediation or closure.
