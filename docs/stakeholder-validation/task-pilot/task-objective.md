# Pilot task objective

## Participant-facing objective

> Using PULSE, review this dependency-release investigation and determine what can defensibly be concluded for the selected repositories. Identify what remains uncertain, what evidence supports or limits your conclusion, and what you would investigate next before taking action.

## Research objective

Observe whether a relevant participant can independently:

- identify the bounded case and repository population;
- interpret finding status and evidence sufficiency;
- distinguish UNKNOWN from bounded NO;
- find provenance and recognize an absent support edge;
- interpret a detector candidate and inherited review without treating it as an incident;
- name a missing prerequisite and a plausible next investigation step;
- calibrate trust and explain workflow or review burden;
- state whether PULSE changed, clarified, or left unchanged their intended action.

The pilot does not measure speed, conversion, preference, product-market fit, ROI, ecosystem incidence, or statistical usability. A positive comment is not task success, and completed navigation is not proof of decision value.
