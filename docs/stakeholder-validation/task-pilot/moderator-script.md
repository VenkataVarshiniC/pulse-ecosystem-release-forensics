# Pilot moderator script

## 1. Neutral introduction

“Thank you for helping evaluate an experimental dependency-investigation workflow. We are evaluating whether the information communicates its limits, not evaluating you. The example uses frozen public evidence. Please do not enter or describe private repository details, credentials, employer information, or identifying information.”

## 2. Background confirmation

Ask:

- “Have you personally worked on a dependency or package upgrade?”
- “What types of evidence have you used in that work?”
- “What role did you play in deciding the next action?”

Record only coded, non-identifying answers.

## 3. Experimental boundary

Say: “PULSE is experimental and read-only in this exercise. Its output may be incomplete. We want to see what you believe it means from the interface itself.”

Do **not** explain UNKNOWN, bounded NO, candidate, inherited review, or the correct case answer.

## 4. Initial intended action

Before opening PULSE, give the release/case framing from [task objective](task-objective.md) and ask: “If this were assigned to you with only that framing, what would you initially investigate or do?” Record the answer in the decision-effect template.

## 5. Give the task

Open `/investigations`, provide [task sheet](task-sheet.md), and ask the participant to think aloud. Do not provide the moderator ground-truth document.

## 6. Observe without leading

Allowed neutral prompts:

- “What are you looking for?”
- “What does that label mean to you?”
- “What led you to that conclusion?”
- “Where would you look next?”
- “Please continue when you are ready.”

Avoid:

- defining a status;
- pointing to a specific repository, finding, button, or source;
- saying an answer is correct or incorrect;
- suggesting that two records should be compared;
- implying that PULSE should change the participant’s decision.

If the participant is blocked by navigation after a reasonable attempt, give only a navigation prompt such as “Return to the selected investigation.” Record the prompt and score the affected dimension no higher than `PARTIAL`. Never give a semantic correction during the task.

## 7. Task completion

After the seven tasks, ask: “What would you do next if this were your repository?” Then record the post-task action before asking reflective questions.

## 8. Post-task questions

Use [post-task questions](post-task-questions.md) in their listed neutral order. Separate direct statements from moderator interpretation.

## 9. Close

Say: “This exercise does not authorize a product or gate decision by itself. We will combine anonymized observations with other evidence.” Remind the participant not to send private follow-up evidence.

## Session controls

Use one moderator and, if available, one silent note-taker. Recommended session length is 35–50 minutes, used only for planning and not as a benchmark. Stop if the wrong snapshot appears, the published data changes, the stack fails materially, or the participant begins disclosing private information.
