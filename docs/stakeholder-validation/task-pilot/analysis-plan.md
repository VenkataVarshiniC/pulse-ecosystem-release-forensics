# Pilot analysis plan

## Inputs

Analyze only completed, privacy-reviewed session records tied to the verified published snapshots. Preserve raw local notes separately and do not commit recordings or identifiers.

## Per-participant analysis

For each participant:

1. verify relevance, case, snapshot, and fair task opportunity;
2. code dimensions A–J using `PASS`, `PARTIAL`, `FAIL`, or `NOT_OBSERVED`;
3. record the observed behavior supporting each code;
4. classify decision effect independently as `CHANGED`, `CLARIFIED`, `UNCHANGED`, or `NOT_OBSERVED`;
5. separate direct statements, moderator prompts, and analyst inference;
6. retain trust, burden, workflow-fit, and negative evidence;
7. identify system limitations that could explain a result.

## Cross-participant analysis

Compare patterns using explicit denominators, for example “2/3 participants,” without inferential statistics, percentages that obscure tiny denominators, weighted scores, averages, or significance tests. Examine:

- scope and status comprehension;
- provenance behavior and unsupported-link inference;
- candidate/review misunderstanding;
- missing-prerequisite and next-action quality;
- trust calibration and review burden;
- decision effect and workflow placement;
- contradictions and severe failures;
- differences associated with relevant experience, without population claims.

## Reconciliation

Integrate task evidence with the cumulative Wave 1/Wave 2 survey record. Reassess hypothesis, requirement, persona/owner, trust, counter-evidence, value, Phase 6 recommendation, Phase 7 decision, and G8. Proposed changes remain versioned; do not rewrite frozen survey evidence.

## Future task-validation classification

- `TASK_VALIDATION_SUPPORTIVE` — Core semantics are understood across comparable sessions, provenance and gaps can be used, and at least some bounded investigation benefit is observed without a severe shared failure.
- `TASK_VALIDATION_MIXED` — Material strengths and weaknesses coexist, participant results diverge, or benefit is limited/unclear.
- `TASK_VALIDATION_CHALLENGING` — Repeated misunderstanding, burden, distrust, or workflow mismatch materially challenges the intended workflow, but repair or further evidence may be plausible.
- `TASK_VALIDATION_FAILED` — The intended bounded workflow cannot be used defensibly in the observed setting or severe semantic failure makes the current design unsafe for its intended decision support.

Classify G8 separately. Completed tasks do not automatically advance it.

## Outputs after real sessions

Create anonymized per-participant records, a cross-participant synthesis, counter-evidence update, decision-effect summary, requirement/persona impacts, task-validation classification, and a separate G8 reassessment. Do not begin Phase 8 unless a later, explicit decision authorizes it.
