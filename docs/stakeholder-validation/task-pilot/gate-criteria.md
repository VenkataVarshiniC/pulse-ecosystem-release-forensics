# Pilot gate criteria

## Current state

G8 remains `PARTIALLY_VALIDATED`. Pilot preparation, recruitment, or session completion does not change the gate automatically.

## Evidence required before considering a stronger gate

A candidate move beyond `PARTIALLY_VALIDATED` requires all of the following evidence to be available for review:

- at least two independent, relevant participants complete comparable core tasks; a one-participant pilot remains directional;
- participants can identify the bounded scope without treating it as ecosystem coverage;
- UNKNOWN and bounded NO are materially understood, with no unmitigated shared inversion of their meanings;
- provenance can be navigated and an absent finding-support edge is not silently filled by assumption;
- participants identify at least one case-relevant missing prerequisite;
- candidate firing, shared artifact, inherited review, and REJECT are not materially confused with accepted incident or absence of friction;
- at least one participant reports an observed investigation benefit such as a changed or clarified next action, with the initial and post-task action recorded;
- investigator, reviewer, and final-owner placement becomes clearer or their continued ambiguity is documented;
- trust, review burden, workflow fit, and negative evidence are retained;
- privacy review and case/snapshot verification pass for every included session;
- requirement and persona implications are reconciled without weakening integrity rules.

These are evidence prerequisites, not an automatic promotion rule. The project owner must assess the full record separately.

## Reasons to retain `PARTIALLY_VALIDATED`

Retain the gate when any material condition applies:

- fewer than two comparable relevant sessions are completed;
- the system or snapshot prevents fair observation of core tasks;
- severe or repeated UNKNOWN/bounded-NO misunderstanding occurs;
- candidate/review semantics are materially misunderstood;
- provenance cannot be traced or participants infer unsupported links;
- no observed investigation benefit is reported;
- workflow placement or decision authority remains too ambiguous;
- counter-evidence indicates excessive burden, distrust, or no useful gap;
- privacy, moderation, or note quality prevents defensible interpretation.

## Upper boundary

This purposive 1–3 participant pilot alone cannot establish `VALIDATED` for a population, product-market fit, adoption, ROI, or production readiness. Any `VALIDATED_WITH_LIMITATIONS` proposal would require a written reconciliation explaining scope, participant relevance, semantic performance, counter-evidence, and remaining limitations.
