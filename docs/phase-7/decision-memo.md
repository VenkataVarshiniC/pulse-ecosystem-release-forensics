# Phase 7 decision memo

## Decision

**Direction: GO_BOUNDED**

**Phase classification: PASS_WITH_LIMITATIONS**

Retain `p7-boundary-anchor-depth-v1` as a separate experimental reconstruction option with its explicit availability and prerequisite contracts. Do not replace the published baseline or promote the Phase 7 measures to production KPIs. The method earned bounded continuation because it produced valid additional pairs and sufficient assessments while preserving provenance and uncertainty; its mixed record-level result and two-case scope rule out an unrestricted GO.

## Decision questions

1. **Did evidence depth improve qualifying evidence?** Yes, narrowly. Two urllib3 members gained a paired pre-release target declaration, which satisfied four member-question prerequisites. Two other members gained pairs without target declarations and therefore gained no analytical sufficiency.
2. **Did historical context improve?** Mixed. Paired records rose from 1 to 5, but unresolved records rose from 9 to 10 because three legacy after-only states became more conservative. Seven of 16 records changed.
3. **Did analytical sufficiency improve?** Yes, from 2/40 to 6/40. UNKNOWN fell from 38 to 34 only where the frozen prerequisites were satisfied. The four new answers are bounded NO.
4. **Did support linkage improve?** No. Supported positives remained zero and support-link coverage remained unavailable at a zero denominator. The architecture correctly blocked fabricated edges.
5. **Did uncertainty semantics remain intact?** Yes. Adoption and recovery stayed UNKNOWN; missing or incomplete evidence did not become NO; structural and parser limits remained explicit.
6. **Did false-positive risk increase?** No observed increase. Candidate/review state stayed 2 candidates, 0 accepted, 2 rejected, and 1 distinct artifact. No new positive finding was created.
7. **Which gaps remain structural?** Installed/resolved-version evidence, eligible recovery sources and denominators, paths outside the frozen pair, exact transition timing, a pre-release feedi anchor, and live positive support-link validation remain unavailable or outside scope.
8. **Should evidence-depth investment continue?** Only in bounded form. Preserve this method and its contracts; any broader source/path/cohort validation needs a separately frozen experiment. Do not spend further within Phase 7.

## Limitations attached to the decision

- Two cases and eight members do not establish ecosystem transferability.
- Boundary snapshots can miss intermediate states and do not identify change time.
- A path pair is not adoption, compatibility, or runtime use.
- Three baseline after-only records became unresolved under stricter evidence fitness.
- Zero accepted events means positive support linkage was verified structurally and by tests, not against a live positive finding.
- Public-source access succeeded for this run, but anonymous access is not a production reliability guarantee.

`G8 = NOT_YET_VALIDATED`. No stakeholder demand, value, ROI, or product acceptance is claimed.

## Recommended next milestone

Close the evidence-method program at this bounded experimental state and move to the separately governed portfolio-closure decision when authorized. Carry the mixed result, method version, zero-positive support-link limitation, and stakeholder hold into that gate. Do not broaden Phase 7 or silently make the treatment the published default.
