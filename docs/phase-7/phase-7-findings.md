# Phase 7 findings

## Experimental finding

The frozen evidence-depth method produced a defensible but limited improvement. Exact-ref paired history rose by four records and lifecycle sufficiency rose by four assessments. This occurred without adding repositories, paths, candidates, accepted reviews, positive findings, or support links.

The result is mixed rather than uniformly positive:

- 4 records changed to `PAIRED`;
- 3 legacy `AFTER_ONLY` records changed to `UNRESOLVED` under stricter evidence fitness;
- 2 members gained qualifying pre-release target context;
- 2 other members gained pairs with no target declaration and no sufficiency benefit;
- 34/40 findings remain `UNKNOWN`;
- all 6 available decisions are bounded NO;
- supported positives and support links remain zero.

## Source results

The collector completed all 48 prescribed attempts: 16 repository-anchor attempts and 32 exact-ref file attempts. Attempt availability was:

| Availability | Count |
| --- | ---: |
| `AVAILABLE` | 27 |
| `NOT_FOUND` | 16 |
| `INCOMPLETE` | 2 |
| `STRUCTURALLY_UNAVAILABLE` | 3 |
| `NOT_PLANNED` | 0 |
| `SOURCE_UNAVAILABLE` | 0 |
| `CONFLICTING` | 0 |

The 3 structural attempts are the missing feedi pre-release anchor and its 2 dependent virtual file attempts. The 2 incomplete attempts are scrapelib pyproject states that could not meet the exhaustive parser contract. Seven history records were absent at both approved exact refs. The method stopped at these states and performed no alternate-path, branch, tag, clone, parent-history, or private-source search.

## Finding semantics

- The 4 new bounded NO findings belong to migration friction and remediation for advent-of-code-data and map-machine.
- No finding states that migration friction or remediation did not occur in reality; each says no accepted qualifying evidence was found inside the complete bounded contract.
- Adoption declarations do not establish adoption. All 16 adoption-readiness/observed-adoption assessments remain UNKNOWN.
- All 8 recovery assessments remain UNKNOWN.
- `REJECT` remains a review decision about a detector firing and does not prove absence of friction.
- Two candidate firings remain one shared artifact, not independent incidents.

## Negative and null results

Support linkage did not improve because there were no accepted positive findings. This is reported as unavailable `0/0`, not success. Candidate yield did not improve and was not a Phase 7 success criterion. The method did not reduce historical unresolved count; it increased it by one while improving paired count, reflecting stricter classification of weak legacy states.
