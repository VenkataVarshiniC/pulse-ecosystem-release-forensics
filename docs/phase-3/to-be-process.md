# Provisional To-Be release-forensics investigation

**Status: design hypothesis pending primary-research reconciliation.** This is a proposed PULSE-supported process, not an observed organizational workflow, stakeholder approval, or a formal requirement. It responds to the [Phase 2 PIVOT](../phase-2/phase-2-findings.md), [Milestone 2 gaps](gap-analysis.md), and the narrow [public bot-PR study](secondary-research/github-workflow-analysis.md). The [current-state process](as-is-process.md) remains separate. PULSE is release-forensics decision support for a declared release and downstream cohort, not a generic dependency updater. Lifecycle categories are evidence categories, not a mandatory chronology or causal chain.

## Proposed path and evidence gates

| Stage | Proposed activity and ownership | Decision or output | Evidence basis / validation status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| 1. Trigger | A human investigator chooses a consequential upstream release or other signal to examine. A major release, known compatibility concern, failed update, operator request, or relevant deprecation/security notice could prompt a case; the actual stakeholder trigger is unknown. | Open a candidate investigation or decline it. | M1 [problem hypothesis](business-problem.md); triggers **require stakeholder validation**. |
| 2. Declare scope | Human supplies target dependency and release/version, bounded repository cohort, release boundary, observation window, and selection rationale. PULSE records the typed case and never silently expands it to an ecosystem. | Frozen investigation boundary and denominator. | Existing typed cases/cohorts are Phase 2 **system-supported**; organizational cohort choice is a **design hypothesis**. |
| 3. Acquire evidence | PULSE collects planned public metadata, issues, PRs, commits, and manifest/path history with authenticated bounded requests, immutable snapshots, task outcomes, provenance, and resumability. Additional downstream-release, resolved-version, and CI/runtime sources are candidate extensions only where access and decision value are established. | Source inventory with `complete`, `partial`, `unavailable`, or `not_collected` per source; failures remain visible. | Core method **system-supported**; new sources and usefulness **unvalidated**. |
| 4. Reconstruct and check sufficiency | PULSE distinguishes current declaration, exact-ref historical declaration, release-boundary context, target-version resolution, and observed behavior. It checks stage-specific prerequisites, conflicts, missing sources, and eligible denominators before findings. | `sufficient`, `incomplete`, `unavailable`, or `conflicting` evidence state for the question asked. Missing or after-only history cannot yield a release-window negative. | [Evidence-sufficiency rules](../phase-2/evidence-sufficiency.md) **system-supported** in bounded cases; method improvement needed for wider coverage. |
| 5. Generate candidates | Deterministic rules surface possible adoption/readiness, friction, remediation, or recovery evidence with exact source and rationale. Copied upstream release text is marked as upstream context, never accepted as downstream effect on text alone. | Candidate ledger, **not accepted lifecycle findings**. | Existing candidate/review separation **system-supported**; signal quality and recall unvalidated. |
| 6. Choose review attention | PULSE proposes an explainable attention queue using declared case relevance, source sufficiency, unresolved conflicts, and candidate rationale. It distinguishes routine update artifacts from cases with downstream-specific evidence or unresolved questions. No opaque score or automated priority mandate. | Suggested review order and reasons; human may override. | **Design hypothesis:** only 6/20 base public PRs had visible human interaction, but this does not prove low effort or a desired triage rule. Cross-repository decision is unobserved. |
| 7. Review evidence | Analyst inspects source text, provenance, relevant diff/history, conflicting evidence, and confidence; records `accept`, `reject`, or `needs more evidence` with reason. Unresolved review blocks acceptance. | Independently reviewed ledger. | Independent review **system-supported**; organizational reviewer identity and acceptable workload **unvalidated**. |
| 8. State repository finding | PULSE derives a bounded stage finding from accepted evidence and prerequisite status: `YES`, `NO QUALIFYING EVIDENCE FOUND` only under the declared complete method, or `UNKNOWN`. A zero-candidate run is not proof of no impact. | Traceable repository report, explicit uncertainty and unavailable metrics. | Deterministic reporting and UNKNOWN **system-supported**; usefulness to users **unvalidated**. |
| 9. Compare declared cohort | A proposed cohort view separates repositories with accepted findings, unresolved evidence, collection failures, and possible attention needs; it shows denominator, confidence/rationale, and source gaps. | Investigation view, not ecosystem prevalence or automated business priority. | Existing cohort reporting **partial**; portfolio use **requires stakeholder validation**. |
| 10. Decide next action / closure | Human owner decides whether to investigate further, ask for missing evidence, defer, or close the bounded investigation. PULSE records what its evidence establishes and what remains outside it. | Case disposition with unresolved items, never automatic production-recovery claim. | Completion owner/criterion **requires stakeholder validation**. |

These stages organize the proposed investigation, not the order in which adoption, friction, remediation, and recovery evidence must occur. A compatibility response may precede a later public friction report. A merged PR, declared dependency, passing check, completed migration, and recovered production service remain distinct observations.

## Decision catalogue (provisional)

| ID / decision | Proposed owner | Evidence input | Outcomes | Automation and uncertainty | Validation |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| TB-D01 / Which case and repositories? | Investigator or accountable owner, identity unknown | Release signal, owned repository list, window | Include, exclude, defer | Human-owned; PULSE records rationale and bounded scope, cannot infer whole ecosystem. | **Requires stakeholder validation**. |
| TB-D02 / Which repository needs deeper attention? | Investigator | Source inventory, candidate rationale, conflicting or missing evidence | Review now, later, or no additional review under bounded method | PULSE decision support only; routine-vs-investigation distinction is a hypothesis. | **Requires stakeholder validation**. |
| TB-D03 / Does an artifact qualify? | Analyst/reviewer | Original artifact, upstream/downstream provenance, history, context | Accept, reject, needs more evidence | Human-owned; PULSE can flag relevance and preserve reasons. | Review separation **system-supported**; reviewer authority **unvalidated**. |
| TB-D04 / Is evidence sufficient for a finding? | PULSE computes declared rule; analyst examines conflicts | Collection outcomes, historical context, review state, source/denominator policy | Sufficient, incomplete, unavailable, conflicting | Automated checks can block unsupported negatives; disputed interpretation stays human-owned. | Bounded checks **system-supported**; expanded method **unvalidated**. |
| TB-D05 / What can be said about lifecycle evidence? | PULSE derives bounded result from reviewed evidence; analyst owns interpretation | Accepted ledger and stage prerequisites | YES, NO QUALIFYING EVIDENCE FOUND under method, UNKNOWN | Deterministic report; no silent UNKNOWN→NO or causal inference. | Current semantics **system-supported**; user value **unvalidated**. |
| TB-D06 / Is investigation complete? | Accountable human owner, unknown | Findings, missing evidence, operational criterion outside PULSE | Close bounded inquiry, request evidence, keep open, escalate | Human-owned; PULSE cannot certify migration or production recovery from PR disposition. | **Requires stakeholder validation**. |

## Exception and feedback paths

- **Required evidence unavailable or collection fails:** retain repository in the declared cohort and relevant denominator, expose task/source status, downgrade sufficiency, and return `UNKNOWN` where prerequisite evidence is missing. Retry collection only within declared bounds; no silent exclusion.
- **Historical anchor absent:** keep release-time exposure unresolved even if the current declaration exists. Ask for an older anchor or alternative point-in-time source; do not infer non-exposure.
- **Manifest, lockfile, or history conflict:** preserve both dated, sourced states. Mark the relevant question `conflicting` and send it to analyst review; do not silently choose one.
- **Copied upstream text resembles local failure:** keep as upstream context or a candidate for review; require downstream-specific corroboration before acceptance. Rejected candidates stay in evaluation output, not the accepted lifecycle ledger.
- **No candidates:** report detector emission as zero and recall as unknown; an impact or adoption negative still needs its own sufficiency rule.
- **Analyst rejects or defers candidate:** record reason and provenance; do not promote it to a finding. A `needs more evidence` decision feeds back to acquisition or to an explicit unresolved result.
- **Completion evidence absent:** report PR disposition, declaration, CI result, and release/recovery evidence separately; leave migration or production recovery `UNKNOWN` until the relevant definition and source exist.

The [process model](to-be-process-model.md) shows these branches. [Capability priorities](capability-prioritization.md) distinguish preserving proven architecture from improving evidence acquisition. A future Milestone 2A must reconcile this design with genuine stakeholder responses before final Phase 3 closure or formal requirements.
