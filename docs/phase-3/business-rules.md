# PULSE evidence-integrity business rules

These rules formalize bounded forensic interpretation, not a stakeholder-approved operating policy. Source shorthand: [P2 findings](../phase-2/phase-2-findings.md), [P2 sufficiency](../phase-2/evidence-sufficiency.md), [Phase 1 methodology](../phase-1/methodology.md), [M1A PR analysis](secondary-research/github-workflow-analysis.md), and [Milestone 3 boundary](solution-boundary.md). `Exception` names a valid bounded alternative, never permission to weaken a claim silently. The [specification](requirements-specification.md) and [AC](acceptance-criteria.md) make each rule testable.

| Rule ID | Rule | Rationale / originating evidence | Requirements affected | Exception or boundary |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| RULE-001 | A verified not-found result for an **optional** planned source shall not by itself fail required collection. | P2 live optional manifest 404s and required/optional task semantics. | FR-002, NFR-004 | A failed **required** task remains a failure; optional not-found does not establish dependency absence. |
| RULE-002 | A current-path not-found or current absence shall not establish historical absence. | P2 path-scoped history misses older unchanged declarations. | FR-004, FR-006 | Exact-ref historical evidence may support a bounded historical statement for its ref/path. |
| RULE-003 | A current declaration shall not be labeled release-time use, target-version installation, runtime compatibility, or observed adoption. | P2 declaration evidence was 2/8, not adoption. | BR-002, FR-004, FR-005, FR-009 | Separate direct evidence may support one of those claims if its own method is sufficient. |
| RULE-004 | A detector candidate shall not be an accepted lifecycle event without independent qualifying review. | P2 two bot-text emissions rejected. | BR-003, FR-007, FR-008, FR-009 | Accepted reviewed evidence may support a bounded YES. |
| RULE-005 | A rejected or unresolved candidate shall not appear in the accepted evidence ledger or support a YES finding. | P2 review separation and report verification. | FR-008, FR-009, FR-010 | It may remain visible as rejected/unresolved for audit. |
| RULE-006 | Zero emitted candidates shall not establish that no lifecycle event occurred or that detector recall is complete. | P2 had zero emissions for one case and no recall denominator. | FR-007, FR-009 | A **bounded** no-qualifying-evidence finding requires independent stage-specific prerequisites. |
| RULE-007 | Copied upstream release-note/changelog text alone shall not establish downstream friction or remediation. | Two P2 firings from one Dependabot PR rejected; copied text common in M1A sample. | FR-007, FR-008, FR-009 | A separate downstream-specific artifact or statement may qualify after review. |
| RULE-008 | Adoption, friction, remediation, and recovery are evidence categories; findings shall not require strict stage chronology or imply causal order from timestamps. | HTTPX/openai-python chronology in Phase 1 methodology. | BR-002, FR-009, FR-010 | Explicit source-backed causality is a separate claim, not implied by category order. |
| RULE-009 | An after-only exact-ref target declaration shall not support a negative release-window friction/remediation finding requiring pre-release paired-path context. | P2 closure audit corrected urllib3 negative denominator. | FR-004, FR-006, FR-009 | It can count as **contextual historical evidence coverage**, not release-boundary proof. |
| RULE-010 | Successful planned-source collection shall not by itself establish analytical source sufficiency, candidate recall, or an event. | P2 core collection 2/2 and 6/6, yet zero accepted events and adoption/recovery unknown. | BR-002, FR-002, FR-006, NFR-004 | A narrower question may be sufficient under its declared method. |
| RULE-011 | Every frozen cohort member shall remain in the declared case and denominator even if collection or history is incomplete. | P2 frozen cohorts and denominator-aware reporting. | BR-001, FR-001, FR-010 | A new case may declare a different cohort with recorded rationale; no silent exclusion from the original. |
| RULE-012 | Recovery shall not be reported as measured without an explicit qualifying event, recovery-eligible set, denominator, and window; PR merge alone is insufficient. | P2 collected no downstream releases/eligible set; M1A shows PR disposition only. | SR-006, FR-012, FR-009, FR-010 | A bounded recovery signal may be stated when its source and eligibility are defined; it is not production recovery unless separately evidenced. |
| RULE-013 | Temporal association alone shall not be worded as causal attribution. | [Project context](../../PROJECT_CONTEXT.md) and Phase 1 chronology. | BR-002, FR-009, FR-010 | Causality would require independent supporting evidence and reviewed wording. |
| RULE-014 | A finding shall retain matching case, task, artifact, candidate, review, and rule identity; identity mismatch or duplicate terminal review blocks release of the affected finding. | P2 hash, fingerprint, and review-ledger checks. | FR-003, FR-008, NFR-001, NFR-003 | An explicitly unresolved result may be emitted with the conflict exposed; never silently repair provenance. |

Rules 001–014 are integrity constraints rooted in observed technical limits or the project's evidence method. Stakeholder preferences may change presentation or source priorities but cannot silently convert UNKNOWN into NO, a candidate into a fact, or PR disposition into recovery.
