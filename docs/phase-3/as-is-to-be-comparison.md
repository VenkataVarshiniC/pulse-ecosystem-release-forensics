# As-Is to provisional To-Be comparison

The [As-Is](as-is-process.md) is a bounded account of a sampled **Dependabot-initiated public PR artifact path**, plus a separate PULSE system overlay; it is not a stakeholder-validated organizational workflow. The [To-Be](to-be-process.md) is a proposed PULSE-supported investigation, **pending primary research**. Benefits below are qualitative *business outcome hypotheses*, not measured improvement or approval.

| Area | Current state | Proposed future state | Expected benefit hypothesis | Evidence basis | Validation status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |

## Business outcome hypotheses and future measure names

The proposed workflow is intended to reduce avoidable evidence reconstruction, make source gaps and conflicts explicit, improve traceability, reduce review of irrelevant candidates, focus attention on repositories needing investigation, and reproduce bounded conclusions. Whether any of these outcomes matters or improves is untested. Possible later measure names are **investigation completion time**, **candidate acceptance rate**, **review burden**, **evidence coverage**, **UNKNOWN rate**, **cases requiring additional evidence**, and **analyst agreement**. No baseline, target, savings estimate, or population rate is asserted. Primary research must first establish the decision, owner, and acceptable tradeoffs.

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | What it would provide | Fit to current evidence | Main limit |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| A — Report-only forensics | Bounded collection, provenance, deterministic repository/cohort summaries; no attention suggestion. | Strong fit to architecture already validated in Phase 2. | Honest reports were UNKNOWN-heavy; they may not help choose the next investigation without added method and user validation. |
| **B — Human-review forensic workflow (provisionally preferred)** | A plus candidate rationale, explainable attention suggestions, explicit gaps/conflicts, and independent human acceptance. | Preserves Phase 2 review boundary and addresses copied-text failures; allows a hypothesis that not every routine PR warrants deep review. | Portfolio priority and acceptable human workload have not been validated; evidence acquisition still needs improvement. |
| C — Highly automated lifecycle classification | PULSE assigns outcomes with minimal human review. | Weak fit: Phase 2 had no accepted events, no recall denominator, sparse history, and unavailable adoption/recovery. | High risk of unsupported negatives, false downstream signals, and unjustified causal or operational claims. |

**Choice is provisional B**, contingent on stakeholder confirmation that a human-led release investigation exists and on evidence-method improvements. If that decision or review model is not real, revisit A or the product thesis rather than treating B as approved. C is not justified by current evidence.
