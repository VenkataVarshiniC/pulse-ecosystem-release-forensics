# Provisional workflow visible in sampled PRs

This is a **public PR artifact workflow**, not a complete stakeholder As-Is process or BPMN model. Stages describe what can be seen; not every PR shows every stage, and review, checks, comments, and revisions can recur in different orders. Counts are kept separate for Sample A (20 base PRs) and purposive Sample B (7 discussion PRs). See [the analysis](github-workflow-analysis.md), [codebook](github-pr-codebook.md), and [source-linked observations](github-pr-observations.jsonl).

| Visible stage | Evidence and bounded count | Exception / confidence | Question for primary research |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Proposal opened | Dependabot opened 20/20 base and 7/7 discussion PRs; each proposed a Python package dependency change. | **High** for these PRs; title search makes this mechanism unrepresentative. No actual schedule/security trigger was established. | Who initiates upgrades in the stakeholder's real workflow, and what triggers them? |

The public record cannot show a portfolio selection step or why these repositories were prioritized. It also cannot establish adoption, remediation, or recovery outside the particular PR artifact. Those questions remain open for the Google Form and interviews.
