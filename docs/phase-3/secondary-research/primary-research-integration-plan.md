# Integrating future primary research

Google Form responses and genuine stakeholder conversations are pending. **None** is represented in this checkpoint. The [frozen public PR frame](github-pr-candidate-frame.json), [coded observations](github-pr-observations.jsonl), and [workflow analysis](github-workflow-analysis.md) remain secondary evidence; they must not be edited retroactively to agree with later responses.

1. Preserve this analysis and its commit identity. Log later corrections to a source or coding error as explicit amendments with reason and date; retain the original sample IDs and protocol.
2. Analyze Google Form responses separately, recording question wording, response window, qualified/nonqualified counts, missing answers, and recruitment limits. Do not infer representativeness from the form's distribution.
3. Analyze consented interviews separately using anonymized participant IDs and [the discovery findings template](../discovery-findings-template.md). Separate a participant's reported past behavior, source artifacts, and analyst inference.
4. Compare each stakeholder-reported decision and step with the PR-visible stages. Record **confirmation**, **contradiction**, and **not comparable/not observed** with links to both evidence sets. Seek disconfirming cases, including workflows already served by existing tools.
5. Reassess stakeholder roles, the provisional primary user, evidence thresholds, UNKNOWN usefulness, and the scope of the business problem. Revise the later As-Is analysis only where genuine stakeholder evidence warrants it. Public PR artifacts alone are not the stakeholder's complete As-Is process.
6. Only after process/root-cause analysis, derive and trace formal requirements to validated needs and evidence. Record requirements changed or rejected by contradictions; preserve open evidence gaps and acceptance questions for later UAT.

Do not combine a base-sample proportion with purposive discussion cases or survey counts. Neither public PRs nor an unqualified self-selected form alone validates user preference, business impact, or a requirement.
