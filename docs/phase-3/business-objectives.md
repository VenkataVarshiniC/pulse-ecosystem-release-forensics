# Provisional business objectives

These objectives are hypotheses for discovery, not agreed targets or formal requirements. The population, baseline, and measurement method are **TBD pending discovery/baseline measurement**.

| Objective to test | Possible evidence of improvement | Open validation issue |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reduce avoidable manual reconstruction of release-impact evidence. | Compare steps and elapsed analyst effort for a real investigation against a baseline. | Does this task recur often enough, and is evidence accessible? |
| Improve traceability of investigation conclusions. | A reviewer can follow a conclusion to its source, collection method, and completeness boundary. | Which proof standard does the actual decision-maker require? |
| Make uncertainty explicit and usable. | Users correctly distinguish missing evidence from evidence of absence and know the next action. | Are UNKNOWN reports actionable, or merely honest but unhelpful? |
| Prioritize repositories requiring analyst attention. | Compare triage decisions and missed/extra reviews on agreed cases. | Is cross-repository prioritization the real job? |
| Reduce review of irrelevant event candidates. | Measure candidate acceptance and time spent reviewing, with recall limits disclosed. | What false-positive and missed-event tradeoff is acceptable? |
| Produce reproducible investigation outputs. | Re-run the same bounded investigation and compare evidence and conclusions. | Does reproducibility matter to the stakeholder's decision or audit? |

Do not set numeric thresholds until genuine discovery identifies the decision, feasible evidence, and current baseline. A technically measurable objective alone does not prove business value.
