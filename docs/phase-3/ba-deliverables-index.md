# Phase 3 BA deliverables index

This index separates **completed documents** from **validated stakeholder findings**. Milestone 1A is bounded secondary research; Milestones 2–5 are provisional where primary research is needed. No document here is a stakeholder signoff or next-phase implementation.

## Business Context & Discovery

- [Phase 3 plan](phase-3-plan.md) states milestone scope and current partial status.
- [Charter](phase-3-charter.md) frames the release-investigation decision and discovery boundaries.
- [Phase 2 BA inputs](phase-2-ba-inputs.md) translates validated technical results into discovery questions.
- [Stakeholder register](stakeholder-register.md) lists possible roles without inventing participation.
- [Primary-user hypothesis](primary-user-hypothesis.md) identifies the proposed cross-repository investigator and alternatives.
- [Business problem](business-problem.md) states the provisional decision problem, not a measured complaint.
- [Business objectives](business-objectives.md) names possible outcomes without invented targets.
- [Assumptions, risks, constraints](assumptions-risks-constraints.md) distinguishes open propositions from Phase 2 limits.
- [Stakeholder-needs hypotheses](stakeholder-needs-hypotheses.md) records N-01–08 for later validation.
- [Scope](scope.md) bounds release forensics and exclusions.
- [Discovery plan](discovery-plan.md) and [findings template](discovery-findings-template.md) prepare genuine primary research without claiming it occurred.
