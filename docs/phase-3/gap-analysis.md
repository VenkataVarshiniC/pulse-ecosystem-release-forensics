# Current-state decision gap analysis

“Needed decision capability” below is **conditional on a real stakeholder decision being confirmed**. These are analytical gaps, not To-Be design choices or final requirements. System evidence comes from [Phase 2 findings](../phase-2/phase-2-findings.md); the public PR context comes from the [Milestone 1A analysis](secondary-research/github-workflow-analysis.md). No row establishes user demand by itself.

| ID | Current capability/process | Conditional decision capability | Gap | Evidence | Validation status |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| GAP-01 | A declared cohort and bounded public-source collector exist. | Identify the actual owned repositories in a decision. | Phase 2 convenience cohorts are research selections, not an organization's decision scope. | Phase 2 cohort limits; [primary-user hypothesis](primary-user-hypothesis.md). | `hypothesized`; owner/population pending. |
| GAP-02 | Current manifests and path-scoped exact-ref history can be read. | Know target dependency state at the relevant release boundary. | Only 2/8 frozen repositories had target declaration evidence; one had the stronger parsed pre-release paired-path anchor. Current state does not establish historical exposure. | [Phase 2 findings](../phase-2/phase-2-findings.md). | `observed` PULSE gap; decision threshold pending. |
| GAP-03 | Declarations and constraints can be parsed. | Know whether a target version was actually installed/resolved, if that matters. | No comprehensive target-version lockfile or ordinary runtime/CI resolution evidence was collected. | [Phase 2 evidence sufficiency](../phase-2/evidence-sufficiency.md). | `observed` missing input; stakeholder need pending. |
| GAP-04 | Text rules emit reviewable candidates. | Distinguish qualifying local friction/remediation from copied upstream context. | Two emissions from one bot PR were rejected; recall is unknown. Public PR bodies often contain copied upstream text. | [Detector evaluation](../phase-2/detector-evaluation.md); [PR codebook](secondary-research/github-pr-codebook.md). | `observed` limited false-positive mechanism; review burden pending. |
| GAP-05 | Collection outcome/provenance and analyst review are preserved. | Decide what to do with an UNKNOWN or incomplete finding. | Reports accurately expose missing data, but actionability has not been tested with users. | [Phase 2 findings](../phase-2/phase-2-findings.md); [needs hypotheses](stakeholder-needs-hypotheses.md). | `observed` output; usefulness `not observable`/pending. |
| GAP-06 | Public PRs expose merge/closure and some reviewer decisions. | Determine migration/production completion, if that is the decision. | PR disposition does not establish installed version or production recovery; downstream releases and eligible denominator were absent in Phase 2. | [PR observations](secondary-research/github-pr-observations.jsonl); [Phase 2 findings](../phase-2/phase-2-findings.md). | `observed` evidence boundary; completion definition pending. |
| GAP-07 | Source-specific evidence can be reported per repository. | Compare and prioritize repositories across an owned portfolio, if such a job exists. | No observed portfolio owner, priority rule, or cross-repository decision. | [Hypothesis comparison](secondary-research/hypothesis-comparison.md). | `not observable`; `pending stakeholder validation`. |
| GAP-08 | Deterministic reports reproduce the bounded evidence record. | Use the report in an actual investigation or review. | No primary evidence yet on trusted sources, preferred presentation, acceptable uncertainty, or review cost. | [Phase 3 charter](phase-3-charter.md); [discovery plan](discovery-plan.md). | `hypothesized` value; pending. |

Do not infer a final capability prescription from this table. A gap may be irrelevant if the stakeholder's decision is different or existing tools already supply the needed evidence.
