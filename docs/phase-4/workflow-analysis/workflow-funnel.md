# Observable PR workflow: branching diagnostic

This is separate from PULSE's forensic evidence funnel. It is a **set of public evidence states**, not a mandatory sequence or conversion path. The frozen base denominator is 20 throughout; counts overlap and are not subtracted step to step.

```text
20 selected PRs opened and represented a Python dependency-file change
  ├─ automated context: 13 copied upstream notes; 16 badge/links
  ├─ human interaction visible: 6
  │    ├─ explicit approvals: 4
  │    └─ substantive downstream technical comment: 1
  ├─ explicit exception labels: 12 (mostly supersession/close handling)
  └─ terminal disposition: 9 merged | 11 closed without merge
```

The seven purposive discussion examples separately show seven visible interactions, four approvals, one substantive technical case, three rebase-request/up-to-date replies, and four merges/three closures. They are **examples**, not a funnel percentage. Automated notes may precede or coexist with any branch; an approval is not mandatory for merge and exception labels can overlap. A merge or closure alone does not establish compatibility, work effort or PULSE recovery.
