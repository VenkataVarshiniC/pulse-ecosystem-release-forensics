# Provisional KPI hierarchy

**Value hypothesis, not a North Star or approved target:** help an investigator reach a defensible repository-level investigation outcome with traceable evidence and explicit uncertainty. Phase 3 has not validated the decision owner or whether this changes action.

| Level | Indicators | Use and interpretation |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Primary analytical health | MET-006 investigation outcome coverage; MET-005 stage sufficiency; MET-012 UNKNOWN rate; MET-009 reviewed-candidate acceptance; MET-021 accepted-finding provenance coverage. | Diagnose whether the bounded method can answer its declared questions. Display numerator, denominator and unavailable state; none alone proves value. |
| Diagnostic | MET-001 core collection, MET-002 historical context, MET-003 target-version evidence, MET-004 source completeness, MET-013 UNKNOWN reasons, MET-010 rejection, MET-016 required-task completion, MET-018 retry, MET-020 replay. | Explain why primary indicators change; compare within case and source plan before cross-case aggregation. |
| Integrity guardrails | Zero unsupported negative findings; zero rejected/unresolved candidate leakage into accepted events; zero accepted findings without provenance; zero frozen cohort members lost; zero false recovery eligibility. | Structural validation targets, **not** user-value KPIs. A nonzero count blocks publication until reconciled. |
| Stakeholder value | Investigation time saved, trust, usefulness, burden tolerance, adoption intent, priority decision quality, completion. | **PENDING STAKEHOLDER / FUTURE PRODUCT VALIDATION**. Define task, audience, baseline, and acceptable tradeoff first; no current target. |

The hierarchy may change after interviews/Form analysis: product-value ranking, priority and completion metrics, trust dimensions, review-burden metric, dashboard audience/order and success thresholds. Evidence-integrity metrics cannot be weakened merely because users prefer a different visual or workflow. See [Phase 3 reconciliation](../phase-3/stakeholder-reconciliation-framework.md).
